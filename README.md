2. How customers order
The customer picks food and adds it to the cart.
They choose Home Delivery or Self Pickup and enter their name and phone number.
Home Delivery: available only within Sylhet City. They choose their area and give a road/house number. An address outside Sylhet City is refused automatically with: "Sorry! YM Cafe currently delivers only within Sylhet City."
Self Pickup: they choose a pickup time. There is no delivery charge and no minimum order.
They pay (cash on delivery or your mobile payment options) and get a tracking code to follow their order live.

The minimum order for home delivery is BDT 300.

3. Your daily routine (admin panel)
Open https://[your-domain]/admin, enter your password and click Unlock portal.
Keep this page open on a phone, tablet or computer during opening hours. When a new order arrives, you hear a beep.
Each order shows the customer's name, a phone number you can tap to call, the address or pickup time, and the items.
Move the order forward as you work: Pending → Preparing → Ready → Out for Delivery → Completed. For pickup orders, "Ready" means ready to collect. The customer sees every change on their tracking page.
Use Cancelled only if you cannot fulfil the order, and call the customer.

At the top of the panel you can see your revenue for the last 7 days and your average order value. Export sales CSV and Export PDF give you reports.

4. Changing your menu (Menu manager)
Add a food item: type the name, price and description, choose the spice level, tick Vegetarian or Chef Special if it applies, click Choose photo, then Add food. It appears on the website immediately.
Out of stock: switch the item off, and customers can no longer order it.
Change a price or remove an item: use the buttons on the item in the Menu manager.
Photo tips: use a clear, well-lit photo of the dish, taken from above or at a slight angle, under 2 MB. Square photos look best.
5. Delivery areas and charges

In Delivery areas and charges, you can add an area, change its delivery fee and its estimated delivery time. Only areas inside Sylhet City can be added.

6. Delivery and pickup settings

In Delivery and pickup settings:

Switch Home delivery on or off, for example when you have no riders.
Switch Pickup on or off.
Set Pickup opens and Pickup closes times, and your café address and phone number, which customers see. Click Save settings. Changes apply immediately.
7. Riders

Use Riders and dispatch to assign an order to a rider. Customers see that their order is out for delivery.
